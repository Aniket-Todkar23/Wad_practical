About Us

Pune Institute of Computer Technology (PICT) is a premier engineering college established in 1983. It is renowned for its excellence in computer engineering, IT, and EnTC.

Vision

Pune Institute of Computer Technology aspires to be the leader in higher education and research of International repute.

Mission

To be the leading and most sought after institute in education & research in holistic development.
